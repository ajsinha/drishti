Alerts

One alert engine. An alert rule watches a number (a BI visual, a measure of a model, a field) or a rule on one entity (a Rachana-EL predicate such as $.utilisation > 0.8), read as you with your masks, on a schedule, after a load or live. When it breaks, an alert is raised for you and told in your inbox (and by mail, a chat bridge, a webhook or a text if the rule asks). Acknowledge it (it is yours), snooze it, or resolve it; it resolves itself when the number comes back.
