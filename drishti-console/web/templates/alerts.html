Alerts

Two kinds. An alert on a number watches a BI visual, a dataset measure or a field and tells you in your inbox when it crosses a limit, moves too far or does not arrive; the number is read as you, with your masks. A rule watches one entity: the server checks it on every change, even when nobody has {{ PRODUCT }} open, and alerts once when it becomes true; it re-arms when it turns false again.
