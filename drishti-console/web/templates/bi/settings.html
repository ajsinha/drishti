BI settings

Business intelligence (Rupaka). Roles hold the powers; the workspace policy says who creates shared workspaces. bi opens BI and published content, bi-author creates and publishes in workspaces, bi-steward certifies, bi-admin manages BI and sees every workspace. A higher power also opens BI. Administrators hold all four.
