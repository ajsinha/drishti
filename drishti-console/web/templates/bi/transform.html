Transform editor

Build a Python Transform step by step. Add the inputs it reads (a pack kind straight through its connector, or a model), then add steps from the snippets or write your own. After every step you see its rows and a profile of every column, worked out on the rows you may see. Save it as two drafts: the Python item and the dataset that runs it.
