1. Data

Drop .jsonl, .jsonl.gz or .ndjson files, or a folder of them. They are uploaded to the server and every record is analysed there (the same pass as drishti.py pack new).

These files are
a new kind: its own pack, Sutras and screens; or a new booking system's source of a kind that exists (a source module)

2. Map

Every record is read again: each native field is matched to a field of the kind by its name, its type, its values and the kind's enums, with a confidence. Change a row's native field, set a constant, or drop it; then Propose again applies your edits.
