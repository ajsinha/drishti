Reports and subscriptions

Everything that runs for you on a schedule, in one list. A scheduled report is a search, run as you and delivered as a CSV file: to the server's reports folder, or posted to a webhook your administrator allows. A subscription is a BI report, page or visual, or a view, sent by email on a schedule (or after the day's data lands), rendered for each recipient with their own masks and row rules. Press Subscribe on a report or a view to make one.
