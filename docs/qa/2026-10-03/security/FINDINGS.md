## What held (all attempted, all refused or safe)

* Open-design / gated-deploy policy: viewers, narrow, masked and anonymous callers cannot save a Sutra (POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE on `/sutras`, trailing slash, `;x=1`, `//`, `%73utras`, three content types), propose, bind, or save a file (403, or 400 for path-parameter and encoded-slash variants); `approve`/`reject`/`withdraw` by non-approvers and non-owners refused; the proposer cannot approve their own proposal with author+approver, admin or all roles (`DRS-2007`); a real approver can and the design shows `live(v1)`; editing after that returns it to `draft`; pack load/unload/registry/admin routes refused to every non-admin with eight method and path variants and four spoofed identity headers.
* Design isolation: 25 endpoints x 6 callers (anonymous, other author, viewer, narrow, approver, admin) all `404 DRS-5006` or 401 with no leak; 22 path, method and header variants; ids 12 hex (48 bits), foreign, unknown and malformed ids answer the same 404; the list shows only your own; the file store escapes owner names and hashes sample names so neither can reach a path; mass-assignment of `owner`, `id`, `status`, `boundFile`, `shareHash`, `rev` through create or PATCH is ignored.
* Share links: payload is Sutra, operations, sample names (S2-10) and owner only; no sample contents or notes; anonymous 401; the token is 192 random bits and only its SHA-256 is stored; flipping, truncating, extending or re-pointing it at another id, or changing the embedded owner (`../`, case, NUL), is 404; revocation, renewal and re-share each kill the previous token at once; a duplicate of a shared design is not shared; the share token grants nothing on the owner endpoints.
* Reference samples and `source:` links: a user who may not open `trade` gets 403/noAccess on preview, check, shape, autodesign, ops preview, export and proposal for refs and for `source: link(..., 'trade')` panels; a demoted owner (same user, narrower role) cannot read the trade reference of their own design through any surface and cannot add one; masked `trader` is bullets in preview, check, auto-design, export, proposal evidence, reviews and versions.
* Pack import: nothing is written to disk (in memory), so zip-slip (`../`, absolute, backslash) entries are skipped and no canary file appeared; 200 MB of zeros (204 KB zip) and 30 MB unpacked answer 413 in 0.1 s; 20 000 entries 413; garbage, empty, wrong content type 400/415; duplicate entries tolerated; billion laughs, 5000-deep flow, 3000-deep block map, `!!java` ProcessBuilder / URLClassLoader tags and a 20 MB scalar are stored as invalid or refused (422/413), the server stays responsive and nothing executed (canary absent).
* Upload limits: 51 samples, 6 MB document, depth 65+, 100 000-deep body, 26 MB body, per-user 50 designs all answered `DRS-5005` 413; non-object, NaN, wrong content type (415), odd names and kinds 400.
* Console: all 15 state-changing `/build` routes refuse `Origin: https://evil.example`, `Origin: null`, a foreign Referer, another port, `localhost` for `127.0.0.1` and a prefix-trick origin (90 rows, 403 `DRS-5002`); the reviews decision form likewise; JSON routes refuse `text/plain`, form, multipart and `text/json` bodies (415) and take `application/json` and `+json`; foreign designs through every console route 404; the share preview of a stored entity says "may not open trade" for qa-narrow; no payload was reflected unescaped in 11 pages with hostile design, sample, Sutra and proposal text (stored-XSS probes), including the review comment and inline init JSON.
* Error bodies: 496 fuzzed requests gave problem+json without class names or absolute paths, except the two bare 500s in S2-07.
* Personal API tokens: read designs, every write refused (403).

## Setup note

The first console run was started without `DRISHTI_AUTH_ENABLED=true`; with `auth.enabled` false every visitor is the desk user with every role (documented). Those rows were discarded and everything above was rerun with sign-in on (`scripts/start_console.sh`).
