Genomics showcase: the mock-ups

Two hero screens, three layouts each, for the product owner's choice. One story joins them: a cohort points at a gene (genomics), the gene's protein is measured change by change and then redesigned (protein engineering). About 200,000 synthetic rows in all. The design is in modules/genomics/docs/guide.md.

Genomics · option A: The linked board recommended

One page, five cards, one selection. The association plot finds the locus; the cohort matrix shows who carries what; the map of samples lets you brush a group; the lollipop places the variants on the protein, coloured by the measured effect of each one. Try: click the HLXT17 peak; click the cluster name "C3 immune-hot"; drag a rectangle on the map; click a lollipop head.

Genomics · option B: The genome browser

Tracks stacked on one genome axis (association, copy number, the locus), with the gene and its cohort in a side panel. Closest to what a geneticist already knows; less of a first-minute impact than option A. Try: click a peak on either track, then a cluster name on the map.

Genomics · option C: The guided story

Four steps, one large visual each, with the sentence the presenter says. Best on a phone and for a first-time presenter; less room to explore. The steps use the same cards as option A, so this can ship as a "guided" mode of A.

Protein engineering · option A: The workbench recommended

The scan on top (what every change does), the structure and the design campaign below (where it is and what we built), the leaderboard at the bottom. One selection runs through all of them. Try: Play the rounds; click a blue cell of the heatmap; click a design in the leaderboard; switch 3D trace to Contact map.

Protein engineering · option B: Structure first

The molecule is the hero: a large 3D trace on the left, the residue and the scan beside it. The most striking still image; the design campaign moves below the fold. Try: drag the molecule, click a residue, then a bar.

Protein engineering · option C: The design race

The campaign is the hero: press Play and watch eight rounds climb the landscape while the winning changes spread. The easiest to narrate to a non-specialist; the scan becomes supporting evidence. Try: Play, then click a row of "Which changes won".
