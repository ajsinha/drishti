Engines menu: option A, a mega-menu with a column per enginerecommended

One top-level Engines menu replaces today's separate VaR and Exposure menus (engines are not common to all users). It is shown only to a role that opens at least one engine and lists only the engines running on this server: a column per engine, its command word in the column head, every page one click away with its command on the right. The top bar becomes Views, Engines, BI, Build, Admin, Help.

How it feels

  • The Admin and Views mega-menu exactly: coloured icon, label, a one-line description, the command on the right.
  • Everything visible at once: 14 pages of 5 engines, one click to any.
  • The command words teach the terminal: VAR, EXP, FRSA, SIMM, MCUBE.

Why choose it

  • No new pattern: the console already has this panel; the build is an engines menu in menus.yaml whose columns come from the running engines.
  • One click to any page on the desktop; the same drawer on the phone.
  • Grows with the search box and wrapping columns to about 8 engines; past that, C's family headings can sit over the columns without changing a row.

Against it

  • At 9 or more running engines the panel wraps to a second row of columns.
  • An engine with one page (MR cube) leaves a short column.
  • Not-running engines take a line at the foot (small, greyed, administrators only).
