Risk measures is gone (no engine opens for this role) and there is no Internal; the counts are of the pages this person can open.

How it feels

  • A front door: search first, then the four pages most people want, then eight clear doors.
  • The subpage is short: 9 pages in 3 groups instead of 60 in a list.
  • The tile look of Home and the lens landings.

Why choose it

  • Exactly the decided structure, nothing to translate.
  • Best on the phone: the tiles stack under the search box.
  • The page counts tell a new user how big each area is.

Against it

  • Moving between sections goes back through the main page (the breadcrumb).
  • Tiles take more room than a list on a large screen.
