What breaks first in words

What breaks first: the heap, this afternoon, when the intraday layer is full. By the 10th batch the layer holds about 1.6 GB (117,000 changed trades × 3 vectors). With 10 dates open the server needs about 9.4 GB of heap and has 8 GB. First symptom: the safety net (automatic, 819 MB) closes the oldest dates and they reopen in 0.1 s from their hot files (people notice nothing); then, past it, long collections and slow answers. Fix: -Xmx10g -Xms10g and drishti.var.open-heap-mb: 3584; RAM (32 GB) already covers it.
Second: disk, in about 7 months. Plan 450 GB, or move the lakes to object storage and keep 150 GB of NVMe for the hot files.
Fine: RAM and cores have room for twice today's requests.
