How it feels

  • A month-end review tool: what changed, where, and why, first.
  • The engine points at what deserves a look.

Why not recommended

  • Most days people pivot one date; compare is a mode, not the home screen.
  • Two columns per figure halve what fits on screen.

Keep from it

  • Compare as A's Compare picker, with this layout.
  • The failing check's three choices, in every playground.
