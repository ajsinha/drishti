Drishti Trading: three landing pages for traders and desk heads

The trading edition and lens, with a green accent. It opens on your books and desk: the signed-in person is mapped to their trader id and desk. Names, desks, books and counterparties are from the demo data; figures are illustrative.

Built pieces it reuses: the blotter commands, positions, trade valuations, VaR at book and desk nodes, limits, intraday VaR, the VaR what-if and the exposure pre-deal check. New: "my" scoping, the day's P&L tiles, the blotter of today's events, and one combined "check a new trade" answer.NEW
