Drishti World: three landing pages to choose from

The home screen for the world edition: the public-data packs (world economy, news and markets, US equities, GLEIF legal entities, funds and ETFs) in one product. Same Drishti terminal look, a teal accent to tell it from Drishti Risk at a glance. Commands are the packs' own (ECON, NEWS, QUOTE, USEQ, LEI, FUND…); figures are illustrative.

The IMF figures and the Yahoo prices in these packs are for internal demonstrations only. Showing Drishti World to anyone outside the team needs a decision first.
