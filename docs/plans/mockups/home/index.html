Drishti Home, three mock-ups

Where every user lands after sign-in. Each page has a role switcher (risk manager, credit officer, admin), light and dark, desktop and phone width, a Needs-you full/empty toggle, a Ctrl or Cmd K command bar, pin, hide and reorder (drag, or the arrow buttons that appear on hover or focus), and Explain on every number. All figures are synthetic; no network, no external fonts or scripts.

Option A

Calm

Greeting and freshness line, a Needs-you strip that disappears when empty, four to six ranked cards in a two-column grid (quiet cards shrink to one line), then Since your last visit, Recent, Pinned and shared.

Best for: Most people. Least to read; the owner brief in its purest form.

Trade-off: Lower density: a risk manager sees fewer numbers at once.

Open Calm Credit officer, dark Admin, phone

Option B

Feed

Three pinned numbers on top, then one timeline of what changed, newest first, each entry expandable to detail, sparkline and Explain; a divider marks your last visit.

Best for: Occasional users and managers who read top to bottom, and phone.

Trade-off: Less at-a-glance comparison; ranking is time and size of change, not layout.

Open Feed Credit officer, dark Admin, phone

Option C

Cockpit

Key-number strip, limits heat list, two-series trend panel, backtest light with 250-day strip, data-load table, alerts column (calm text when empty).

Best for: Risk managers and analysts who live in the page.

Trade-off: Denser; needs a width of about 1000 px, collapses to one column below.

Open Cockpit Credit officer, dark Admin, phone

Decisions for the owner

  1. Pick one option or a mix (for example A for most roles, C for risk managers by role setting).
  2. Default for a new user: all role cards ranked, or pre-pinned three?
  3. Is Explain a side panel (shown) or an in-place expansion?
  4. Does Needs you allow snooze (shown) or only act and dismiss?
