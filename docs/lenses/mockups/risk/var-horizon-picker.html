VaR horizon picker: mock-up

A segmented control beside the currency picker on the VaR page. Choosing a horizon redraws every panel (headline VaR and ES, limit use, components, contributions, the P&L path) for that horizon and method. The choice is remembered per person and kept in the address. VAR BANK 1-day is the demo's real figure (USD 112,041,646); √10 is exactly 112.04m × 3.162; the 10-day sum figure is illustrative.
