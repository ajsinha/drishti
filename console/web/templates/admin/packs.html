Packs

{% with tab='packs' %}{% include "admin/_nav.html" %}{% endwith %}

A pack brings a domain: its kinds, mnemonics, layouts and connectors. Switch a loaded pack off and, at everyone's next click, its kinds cannot be opened, its mnemonics and suggestions disappear, and it leaves every pack menu. Switch it on and it all returns. A pack another pack builds on stays on until that one is off; switching a pack on switches on what it builds on. The choice is kept across restarts and recorded in the audit log.
