New screen

Two steps: bring data (any mix of the five ways below), then choose what to start from. What you bring is kept on the server in a design that only you can open, so you can leave and come back (see My designs). An unnamed design is forgotten after a day; a named one after 90 days untouched.

    Start from a schema alone: sample documents are generated from its types, enums, formats, examples and bounds. They are labelled synthetic and are not evidence that a screen works on real data.

    Entities from a store

    Kept as references and read again each time with your current rights and field masks, never copied. A kind you may not open is shown as no access.
